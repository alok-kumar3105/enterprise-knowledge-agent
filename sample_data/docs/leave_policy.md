# Helios Robotics Leave Policy

Helios Robotics is a fictional company used for demonstration purposes. Policy code HR-LV-2026.

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly at 2 days per month. Part-time employees accrue leave pro rata based on contracted hours.

Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March if not used.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive working days.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to 26 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers are entitled to 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Parental leave must begin within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Requesting Leave

Leave requests are submitted through the PeoplePortal at least 14 days in advance for planned absences of more than 3 days. Managers must approve or decline a request within 5 working days.
