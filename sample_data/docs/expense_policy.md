# Helios Robotics Travel and Expense Policy

Policy code FIN-EXP-2026. Applies to all employees and contractors travelling on company business.

## Flights

Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class requires written approval from a Vice President.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is 180 USD in standard cities and 260 USD in high-cost cities such as New York, London, Singapore and Bengaluru city centre.

## Meals

The daily meal allowance while travelling is 75 USD. Alcohol is not reimbursable except at approved client entertainment events.

## Submitting Expenses

Expense reports must be submitted in ExpenseHub within 30 days of the expense date, with itemised receipts for every item above 25 USD. Reports submitted after 90 days will not be reimbursed. Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle.
