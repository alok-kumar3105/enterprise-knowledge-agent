# Helios Product FAQ

## What products does Helios sell?

Helios sells three autonomous robots: the Atlas-W warehouse picker, the Orion-D delivery rover and the Lyra-C cleaning robot.

## What is the warranty period?

Atlas-W and Orion-D include a 3-year limited hardware warranty. Lyra-C includes a 2-year limited hardware warranty. Extended warranty plans add up to 2 additional years.

## What are the support tiers?

Standard support covers business hours with a first response within 8 business hours. Premium support is available 24/7 with a first response within 1 hour for critical (P1) incidents.

## How often is the robot software updated?

Over-the-air software updates ship every 6 weeks. Security patches are released as needed and installed automatically within 72 hours.

## What is the battery life of Orion-D?

Orion-D runs for up to 10 hours on a single charge and recharges to 80 percent in 90 minutes.
