# New Engineer Onboarding Guide

## First Week

New engineers receive their laptop on day one and are assigned an onboarding buddy from their team. By the end of the first week every engineer should have shipped one small change to production.

## Code Review Standards

Every change requires approval from at least one reviewer who owns the affected code. Changes touching robot safety systems require two reviewers, one of whom must be from the Safety Engineering team. Pull requests should stay under 400 changed lines where possible.

## On-call

Engineers join the on-call rotation after 90 days. On-call shifts last one week and are compensated with one extra day of leave per shift.

## Learning Budget

Each engineer has an annual learning budget of 1,500 USD for courses, books and conferences, approved by their manager.
